- **Added:** `lodestar-core` can now route `check_claim_overlap` through a federated repository's Ackplane claim registry instead of the local `tasks` table (ADR-0096 clause 5). `Lodestar::with_federated_claim_source` injects a `FederatedClaimSource` implementation; without one (the default), overlap detection is exactly today's local-table behavior. The concrete Ackplane RPC client that would populate this seam is separate follow-on work — this crate stays local and stdio-only (ADR-0004).
