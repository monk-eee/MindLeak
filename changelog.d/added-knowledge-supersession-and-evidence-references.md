- **An active knowledge statement can now be superseded by a newly-recorded replacement, and every statement can accumulate a bounded, typed evidence trail (ADR-0113 decisions 1, 3, and 7).** `supersede` inserts the replacement directly as Active and marks the prior statement `Superseded` — a third lifecycle state distinct from retirement, since the prior row is preserved exactly as it stood (`retired_at` stays unset) and instead gains a `superseded_by` pointer. A non-empty reason recording why the replacement won is required, and every accepted supersession appends one immutable `knowledge_supersessions` receipt. Separately, `record_evidence_reference` attaches a task, context packet, validation run, or receipt to a statement in any lifecycle state, tagged with whether it corroborates or contradicts — recorded as separate facts, never folded into one confidence number — and `evidence_references` reads them back recency-first, hard-bounded at 100 rows regardless of the requested limit.
