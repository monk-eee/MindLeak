- **Added `ClaimDelegationService.RecoverClaim` (ADR-0096 wire contract, slice 4).** A node can explicitly take over a claim it believes is stranded (`rpc RecoverClaim(ClaimRecoverRequest) returns (ClaimLeaseResult)`), naming both `expected_owner` (the current holder it believes it is superseding) and a mandatory `reason` recorded in `delegated_claim_history` for audit. Recovery only succeeds once the lease has genuinely expired -- a live lease is never recoverable out from under its holder (ADR-0096 clause 6, "holed, not extended") -- and a mismatched `expected_owner` is refused rather than blindly overwritten, protecting against a caller taking over a different claim than it believes it is. A same-owner recovery preserves `claim_started_at` and `branch`, exactly as `DelegateClaim`'s same-owner reclaim does (ADR-0048); a different owner resets both and records the lapse.
