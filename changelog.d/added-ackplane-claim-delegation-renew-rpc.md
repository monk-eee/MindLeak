- **Added `ClaimDelegationService.RenewClaim` (ADR-0096 wire contract, slice 3).** A node with a still-live delegated claim lease can extend it (`rpc RenewClaim(ClaimRenewRequest) returns (ClaimLeaseResult)`) without losing `claim_started_at`, `branch`, `paths`, or `symbols` -- only `lease_expires_at` moves, matching `lodestar-core`'s `renew_lease` semantics exactly. A renew from a non-owner, or against an already-expired lease, is rejected: an expired lease needs a fresh `DelegateClaim`, not a renewal. `ClaimStore::renew` reuses the existing owner-guarded compare-and-swap pattern and records every attempt in `delegated_claim_history` via the existing Granted/Rejected outcome tags.
