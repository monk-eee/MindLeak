- ADR-0146 is proposed, supplying the inbound half ADR-0141 assumed but never specified. ADR-0141 required the server to report "the highest supervisor frame sequence it has durably accepted", but nothing put that sequence on the wire: the outbox allocates it locally as `positions().last_enqueued + 1`, and `DirectiveReceipt`'s `directive_sequence` is the server-issued directive's number, not the supervisor's frame sequence. ADR-0146 has the supervisor stamp `outbox_sequence` on the frames its outbox carries, so the server's answer becomes an acknowledgement of what the supervisor actually stated rather than a number the server derived. It rejects a server-side count of accepted frames explicitly, since that matches only through an unstated invariant and diverges silently once the outbox carries more than directive receipts. A frame sent outside the outbox carries no sequence, and absent there means "not part of the resendable stream" rather than "sequence zero". The server's reply rides on `SupervisorFrameReceipt` rather than `HelloAccepted`, because `Hello` identifies only `producer_id` while `supervisor_id` first arrives in the registration frame. Both halves must land together, and either side missing leaves the reconciliation unrun rather than producing a verdict.
