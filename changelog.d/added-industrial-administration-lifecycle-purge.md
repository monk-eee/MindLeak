### Added

- Added Lifecycle purge, the second privileged Industrial Administration capability, under ADR-0119 decisions 1, 7, and 9: a two-phase preview/confirm workflow against one closed data category today (`telemetry_events`), gated by its own adopted, tenant-scoped `LifecyclePurge` policy. `POST /api/v1/repositories/:repository_id/administration/purges` refuses (409, no request row created) without an active policy, otherwise counts matching rows and records a bounded, expiring, idempotent preview. `POST .../purges/:request_id/confirm` executes a single scoped `DELETE ... WHERE tenant_id = $1 AND repository_id = $2 AND occurred_at < $3` against exactly the previewed scope and cutoff — never `TRUNCATE`, `DROP DATABASE`, or a schema-wide statement — and is idempotent; confirming after the window expires or after the authorizing policy is revoked still returns a receipt (`expired`/`refused`) rather than deleting anything. `GET .../purges/:request_id` reads back the receipt. Every route is tenant- and repository-scoped: a request or receipt is only ever disclosed to the principal and repository that made it. The Administration capability list now reports `lifecycle_purge` dynamically (unavailable/refused/available) instead of a static refusal.
