- **`NodeEnrollmentService` now answers `CheckEnrollmentStatus` (ADR-0122).** A caller proves possession of a specific candidate key and learns whether that exact (node, key) binding is enrolled right now, including its full real lifecycle state -- pending, approved, activating, active, expired, or revoked. Every verification failure -- an unrecognised binding, a bad signature, a stale timestamp, a replayed nonce -- collapses to the identical unverified result, so a caller can never enumerate which failure occurred.
