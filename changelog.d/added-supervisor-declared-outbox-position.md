- **A supervisor now states its own outbox position, and Ackplane reports back the highest it has durably accepted.** Outbox-carried frames carry an `outbox_sequence` the supervisor assigns, Ackplane records the maximum per `(tenant, repository, supervisor)` and answers with it on the registration receipt, and the supervisor reconciles the two independently asserted numbers. A supervisor whose durable record was restored from an older copy or truncated is now told so and stops, instead of silently resuming — ADR-0116 decision 7's `IncompleteEvidence` case was previously unreachable because the only position available was the server echoing the client's own number back. Both halves land together (ADR-0141 with ADR-0146); when either side states no position the reconciliation is skipped rather than decided against a fabricated zero.
