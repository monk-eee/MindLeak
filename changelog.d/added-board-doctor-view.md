- **Board Doctor is its own Bridge tab, with an analysis control and a results panel (ADR-0120 decision 7).** A deterministic diagnostic over the Work namespace and claim leases: a live claim with no Work task, a duplicate title under the same goal, an impossible state/lease combination, a long-unanswered wait, and overlapping declared scope are all read-only findings, never a mutation. It is deliberately a separate tab from Work rather than folded into it, since diagnosing the board and browsing its tasks are different operator workflows.
