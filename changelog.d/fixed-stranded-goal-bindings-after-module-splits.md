- Repaired two goal bindings stranded by module splits. A binding names a path, so splitting a bound `X.rs` into `X/mod.rs` plus siblings leaves the binding pointing at a file that no longer exists and every descendant governed by nothing. `crates/ackplane-bridge/src/administration.rs` and `crates/ackplane-server/src/work_command_store/model.rs` were both in that state; the six `administration/*` descendants are now bound to the goal their predecessor held and both dead paths are unbound. `binding-audit` goes from 36 unbound source files and 2 stale bindings to 30 and 0. Recorded in `gaps.d` rather than treated as closed: the `rust-module-length` control encourages exactly the split that causes this, so it recurs until a guard notices one.
