### Added

- Added Recovery inspection, the third Industrial Administration capability under ADR-0119 decision 6: a read-only report against one identified, previously succeeded Snapshot artifact, requiring no adopted policy (decision 2 never lists inspection among the privileged classes). `POST /api/v1/administration/snapshots/:request_id/inspect` reads the artifact, recomputes and compares its manifest digest, decrypts it in-process with the installation's own key, and runs `pg_restore --list` against the decrypted archive to confirm it is well-formed — never touching `ACKPLANE_DATABASE_URL`. A tampered, undecryptable, or corrupt artifact is reported as an independent finding (`integrity_verified`/`decryption_verified`/`archive_valid`), not an error, and durably recorded; each call appends its own immutable report rather than replaying one. `GET .../inspect` reads back the most recent report. Refuses (`409`) when the named Snapshot request has no succeeded receipt to inspect. Tenant-scoped like every other Snapshot/purge route.
