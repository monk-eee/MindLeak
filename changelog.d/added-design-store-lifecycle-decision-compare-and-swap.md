- **`DesignStore::record_decision` now compare-and-swaps the observed lifecycle state (ADR-0123).** `RecordDecisionRequest` gains `expected_lifecycle_state`; the guarded update only lands if the stored design's `lifecycle_state` still matches what the caller last observed, returning `LifecycleStateConflict` on a stale submission and `UnknownDesign` for an unrecognised `design_id` (previously a raw foreign-key error). This closes a real two-writer race the store had no defense against before -- two concurrent decisions against the same design could previously both land silently.
