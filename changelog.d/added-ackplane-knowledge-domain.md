- **Added:** Ackplane can now record, semantically recall, and retire learned-knowledge statements through a versioned `KnowledgeService` gRPC contract, backed by PostgreSQL and pgvector -- effective weight decays by the same formula as the local planes, computed at read time, and recall ranks by real cosine similarity (`<=>`) when an embedding is supplied, never by pulling vectors into application memory for a cosine loop (ADR-0106 decision 3). This is the first slice of the knowledge domain; governance, context, decision, directive, and outcome domains remain separate future work. The RPCs are unauthenticated in this slice -- see `gaps.d/ackplane-knowledge-service-rpcs-are-unauthenticated.md`.
