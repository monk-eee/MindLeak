- **Added three Bridge Design mutations: propose, record a decision, record a materialization (ADR-0123).** `POST /api/v1/repositories/:repository_id/designs` proposes a design (`DesignStore::create_design`'s existing idempotent-insert guarantee is the safety net); `POST .../designs/:design_id/decisions` records a lifecycle decision, protected by the new compare-and-swap on `expected_lifecycle_state` (a stale submission returns `409`); `POST .../designs/:design_id/materializations` records a materialization revision (`MaterializationStore::record_materialization`'s existing idempotency-key contract is the safety net). Each mirrors ADR-0111's `recover` precedent: safety comes from the store's own check, not a caller identity Bridge does not have. The `/design` page gains a propose form and, in each design's detail view, decision and materialization forms.
