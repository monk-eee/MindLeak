- **Added:** Ackplane hosts a read-only projection of a repository's own authoritative local Lodestar constitution through a new `ConstitutionService` gRPC contract: `PublishConstitutionSnapshot` (called by that repository's own tooling) and `GetActiveConstitution`, surfaced as a new read-only tab in the Bridge (ADR-0106 decision 3). A publish replaces the tenant/repository's snapshot wholesale in one transaction -- never an incremental diff -- since a constitution version is immutable at the source. No adopt/tailor/reject/promote/waiver action is exposed here; those remain local-only Lodestar actions. Both RPCs authenticate every request with a signed, domain-separated `ConstitutionAuthentication`, mirroring `KnowledgeService`'s own operation-signing pattern (ADR-0108) with its own domain separator and nonce table.
