- **`ClaimDelegationService` can now release a federated claim voluntarily, before its lease naturally expires (ADR-0096).** Previously the only RPC was `DelegateClaim` (grant/renew); a node that finished its work early had no way to give a task back, so every other federated node was locked out for the full remaining lease. `ReleaseClaim` is owner-guarded and holes the lease immediately rather than deleting the row, so the next `DelegateClaim` call grants it right away. A release by a non-owner, or of an already-expired claim, is a safe no-op. Every attempt is recorded in `delegated_claim_history`.
