- **Fixed a rare Postgres-gated `signing_keys` race in `ackplane-client`'s arbitration suite.** `task_claim_round_trips_through_a_real_ackplane_deployment` and `a_parked_claim_blocks_delegate_and_only_its_owner_may_answer` both register the same fixture identity, and two concurrent first-time inserts of that identical row could occasionally have the loser caught by the table's `(tenant_id, repository_id, node_id, public_key_fingerprint, activated_at)` unique constraint instead of the harmless no-op `ON CONFLICT (signing_key_id) DO NOTHING` is meant to give it -- reproduced 1 time in 60 concurrent attempts against the unfixed code, matching the one real CI failure it caused. `register_test_key` now gates its insert behind a `OnceCell` so only the first caller ever attempts it; a new regression test races many concurrent registrations against a private identity of its own (so it cannot disturb the two tests above) to guard against this.
