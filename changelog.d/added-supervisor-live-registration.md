- **A supervisor can now register, open a session, and heartbeat over one live authenticated Ackplane connection, and the read-only Bridge dashboard reflects all three.** A refusal after the handshake -- a session naming a supervisor that was never registered, say -- now reaches the client as a typed `ClientError::FrameRefused` carrying Ackplane's own `RejectionReason`, rather than as a raw frame the caller had to recognise by hand. It is deliberately distinct from `ClientError::ConnectionRefused`: the connection is healthy, so a caller that conflated the two would reconnect over a fact the server will refuse just as firmly next time. Directive delivery and reconnect reconciliation remain out of scope.
