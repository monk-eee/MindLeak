- **`ackplane-client` can now ask whether a candidate node is enrolled.** A new `EnrollmentClient` (mirroring `ClaimClient`'s shape) wraps ADR-0122's `NodeEnrollmentService.CheckEnrollmentStatus` RPC, so a repository can tell an unreachable arbiter apart from a reachable one that does not recognise it — closing the wire-contract half of `gaps.d/ackplane-client-cannot-detect-unenrolled-repositories.md`. Sourcing a repository's own candidate identity to ask the question on its own behalf remains a separate, undecided piece of work.
