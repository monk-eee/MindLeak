### Added

- Constitution proposals, design materializations, and industrial designs
  now accept and store a caller-supplied, optional `display_label` ("who to
  show in the UI"), stored separately from and never substituted for the
  verified principal now recorded as the authoritative `author`/`actor`/
  `proposed_by` (ADR-0142 decision 4). All three tables the decision named
  are now covered.
