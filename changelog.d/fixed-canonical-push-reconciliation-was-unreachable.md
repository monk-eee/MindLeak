- **Fixed:** `canonical-push`'s claim gate can once again recognize that a branch was already delivered by a completed task. Its board fetch excludes terminal tasks to stay small, so `reconciliationOf` could never find the `done`/`abandoned` task it needs — every reconciliation silently fell through to "no live Lodestar claim". `task_query(view="board")` gained an optional `branch` filter (independent of `include_terminal`, small regardless of ledger history size) so the claim gate can ask the narrow question it actually needs instead of widening to the whole terminal history.
