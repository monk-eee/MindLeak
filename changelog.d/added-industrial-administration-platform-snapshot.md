### Added

- Added the first privileged Industrial Administration capability: a platform-scoped Snapshot, authorized under ADR-0128's recognition of the hardened loopback developer profile as ADR-0119's verified principal and gated by an explicitly adopted `AdministrationPolicy` (`POST /api/v1/administration/policies`) naming its operation, classification, retention basis, and lifetime. `POST /api/v1/administration/snapshots` runs `pg_dump --format=custom` against the deployment's own database, encrypts the artifact with a locally-generated key (`ACKPLANE_SNAPSHOT_KEY_PATH`, generated the same way as the Bridge's own tenant salt) under `ACKPLANE_SNAPSHOT_DIR`, and records an immutable, idempotent receipt (`GET /api/v1/administration/snapshots/:request_id`) naming its outcome, artifact path, manifest digest, encryption key id, size, and verification state. Snapshot reports `unavailable` when `ACKPLANE_SNAPSHOT_DIR` is unset and `refused` when no active policy authorizes it. Export, recovery execution, and lifecycle purge remain unimplemented and report so. The Administration page gained matching policy-adoption and Snapshot-request forms.
