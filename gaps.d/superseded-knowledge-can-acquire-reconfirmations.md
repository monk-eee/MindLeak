- **Superseded Ackplane knowledge can be reconfirmed as though it were active -- MEASURED 2026-08-26, OPEN.** `crates/ackplane-server/src/knowledge_store/reconfirmation.rs` documents reconfirming an active statement but guards its update only with `retired_at IS NULL`. `KnowledgeLifecycleState::Superseded` deliberately leaves `retired_at` null, so after an active statement is superseded, `reconfirm` refreshes the obsolete row's `confirmed_at` and appends a durable reconfirmation receipt to it. The adjacent activation and supersession transitions correctly guard lifecycle state; reconfirmation has no superseded-case test. Impact: the audit chain can report fresh corroboration for a statement that has been explicitly replaced, confusing recall and review of the authoritative knowledge.
